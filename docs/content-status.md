# Content status and launch checks

Updated: 20 October 2026.

## Content source

The website content is based on the current [RMB Groundworks website](https://www.rmbgroundworks.co.uk/) and the repository's research checklist. Company details are first-party published statements, not an independent audit of delivery capability.

The build uses Poppins for headings/navigation and Inter for body copy, preserves the existing red identity and logo artwork, and uses imagery from the original RMB site.

## Included content

- Five service groups retain all nine original service labels: foundations and substructure; piling and ground beams; block and beam floors; basement civil works; foul and surface water drainage; demolition; car parks; kerbing and path edging; hard landscaping and external works.
- Six **Work in focus** collections organise original photographs by service. They are explicitly visual collections, not named case studies or evidence of specific client outcomes.
- The published St Albans contact details and Hertfordshire, London and Home Counties coverage are retained.
- The five-stage working process is expanded into concise customer-facing guidance.
- Questions focus on enquiry information and scope; they do not invent prices, programme promises, guarantees or design responsibility.

## Deliberately excluded pending confirmation

- Testimonials attributed on the old site to Jarvis Homes, St Albans City and District Council, and Newbuss Construction. Obtain originals, context, permission and approved attribution before adding them.
- Accreditation badges, insurance amounts, training qualifications, safety records, years of experience, fleet/team size, project counts and guarantees.
- A company registration number or registered-office statement. The association between the site and the candidate Companies House record remains unconfirmed.
- Named clients, site locations, project dates, contract values and measured outcomes for gallery images.
- Unverified social accounts, emergency response claims, quotation turnaround promises and coverage claims for individual towns.

- [ ] Confirm current service scope, in-house/subcontracted delivery, package exclusions and specialist interfaces.

- [ ] Provide named case-study facts if the collections are to become documented project stories. Until then, keep the visual-collection wording.
- [ ] Approve form recipient, processing/delivery provider, retention, privacy information and operational follow-up.
- [ ] Verify a controlled enquiry reaches the intended recipient and that failure/retry states remain usable.
- [ ] Approve any analytics/storage tools and the applicable consent or exemption treatment before activation.

## Content maintenance

Service and work-collection content lives in `src/data/site.ts`. Replace descriptions and captions when better first-party information is available. Do not infer that adjacent images show the same site or use an incorporation date as proof of a team's experience.

Update the content record when a claim is verified, recording its source, approval and any relevant expiry/review date. Keep website statements distinct from independently verified evidence and from proposed future capability.
